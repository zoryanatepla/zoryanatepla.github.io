What to know

  • Brackets are attached to the teeth and work with an orthodontic wire and other components as clinically indicated.

  • We choose the appliance according to diagnosis, hygiene, aesthetic preferences and clinical suitability.

  • The plan includes preparation of the mouth, monitoring visits and a retention phase after active treatment.

Important details

Careful hygiene and following dietary advice are important during treatment. Temporary discomfort and individual risks are possible; we discuss them before starting.

Treatment duration and cost can be discussed after assessment and agreement on an individual plan. This page does not replace an in-person consultation.

Before your first visit

A little preparation.
More peace of mind.

01

Tell me what matters

What would you like to change? What concerns you? Think about your expectations to help us have a focused conversation.

02

Bring what you already have

Existing scans and previous reports may be useful. You do not need to arrange new investigations before your consultation.

03

Make room for questions

Write down what you would like to know about methods, care, follow-up visits and retention. Understanding your plan matters as much as following it.
