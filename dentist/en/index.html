Answers to your questions

More clarity.
Less worry.

It all starts with a conversation. Here are answers to questions that often come up before a first visit.

Questions are welcome

Understanding helps you make informed decisions. There is always room for important questions.

A first preventive check-up is usually recommended around age 7, when permanent teeth begin replacing baby teeth. If you notice difficulties chewing, pronounced asymmetry, mouth breathing or other concerns, seek advice earlier. A check-up does not always mean starting treatment; sometimes observation is enough.

Braces are fixed, while aligners are removable clear trays that are changed in sequence. Aligners require consistent wear according to the prescribed schedule. The choice depends on your bite, the complexity of tooth movements, oral health and your ability to follow the plan. Both methods require assessment and professional monitoring.

Orthodontic treatment is possible in adulthood. The condition of your teeth, gums and supporting bone, as well as general health, matters. Some cases require collaboration with other specialists. Suitability, limitations and expected changes are assessed individually after examination.

Duration depends on the diagnosis, age, case complexity, method and adherence to recommendations. An initial estimate can be discussed after assessment and may change during treatment. The active phase is usually followed by retention to help maintain tooth positions.

Yes. Examination, photographs and digital models or impressions help inform the plan. X-rays and other investigations are prescribed only when clinically indicated. Do not arrange a CT scan or other imaging yourself before the consultation; the doctor will determine what is needed.

If moving your jaw has become uncomfortable or painful, discuss it with a clinician. During a consultation, you can describe what you feel while the clinician assesses the joint and chewing muscles. The examination will help clarify whether further assessment is needed and which next step is appropriate for you.
