Respecting your privacy

Privacy
policy.

Updated 5 November 2026

This website presents Dr Zoryana Mykolaivna Tepla, an orthodontist in Kyiv. This policy explains how the website handles visitors’ contact details.

Information we request

The consultation form asks for your name, phone number, whether the consultation is for a child or an adult, reason for enquiry, an optional short comment and consent to data processing. Do not include diagnoses, medical records, health information, or a child’s name or other personal details. A child’s legal representative should provide their own contact details when booking.

Why we need your details

Your details are used to respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation. The legal basis is your voluntary consent, given through a separate checkbox on the form. The details are not intended for marketing messages.

How enquiries are sent

After you fill in the form, the website prepares a request preview. You can copy it or open the doctor’s Telegram chat with a prepared message. The website does not send the request automatically: you confirm sending in Telegram.

Once Telegram opens, its privacy policy applies. If the app does not insert the text, copy the request and paste it into the chat. Telegram privacy policy.

Storage and protection

The website does not write form contents to URLs, localStorage, cookies or public files. Entries remain in page memory until the page is closed or reloaded. Submitted requests are processed by the recipient and delivery service; access should be restricted, and information deleted when it is no longer needed to answer the enquiry or arrange the consultation, subject to legal retention requirements.

Cookies and external services

The website has no advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Fonts and illustrations are served with the website. The hosting provider may keep technical request logs under its own policy. Links to Instagram and external services open third-party websites with their own privacy rules.

Only your choice of light or dark theme is saved in the browser. The page address determines the language. Form entries are not stored in these preferences.

The Contacts section embeds Google Maps. Loading it may send technical data, including your IP address, to Google; Google’s privacy policy applies.

Your rights and contact

You may request information about the processing of your enquiry, ask for correction or deletion, or withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing. Contact the doctor using the public phone number below. Do not send medical documents in public messages.

Medical information

Website materials provide general information and are not remote diagnosis or individual medical advice. An in-person consultation is required for diagnosis and treatment planning.
